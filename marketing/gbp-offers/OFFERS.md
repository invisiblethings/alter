# Google Business Profile offers

Five offers for **Alter Phase Roofing Contracting**, each with an image in this folder (1200×900, all text inside the square crop area) and every field Google asks for.

**How to publish:** Google Business Profile → **Add offer** (or Add update → Offer) → upload the image → fill in the fields below.

**Before you post:**
- **Dates:** set to Nov 1 – Dec 31, 2026. Change them if you want a different window. Google hides an offer after its end date, so repost it with new dates to keep it running.
- **Coupon codes:** they're just for tracking. When a customer mentions one, you know which offer brought them in. Tell whoever answers the phone what each code means.
- **No phone numbers in the text** (Google may remove posts that include them). Customers call from your profile's Call button.
- **Links:** each carries `utm_source=gbp`, so website leads from offers show "gbp / organic" in your lead emails, plus the offer name.

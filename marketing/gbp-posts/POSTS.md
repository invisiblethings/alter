# Google Business Profile posts

Nine ready-to-publish posts for **Alter Phase Roofing Contracting**. Each has an image in this folder (1200×900, the size Google recommends for posts), the text to paste, and the button to choose.

**How to publish:** Google Business Profile → **Add update** → upload the image → paste the text → **Add a button** → choose the button and paste the link.

**Rules followed so Google doesn't reject them:**
- No phone numbers in the text or images. Use the **Call now** button instead.
- No ALL CAPS and no gimmicky symbols.
- Each post is under 1,500 characters.

**Tracking:** each link carries `utm_source=gbp`, so leads from these posts show "gbp / organic" as the source in your lead emails and in GA4.

**Schedule:** post one a week (Monday or Tuesday morning works well). Post 08 is seasonal, so publish it first, before the cold weather.

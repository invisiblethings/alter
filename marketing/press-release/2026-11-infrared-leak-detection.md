**STATEN ISLAND, N.Y., November 2026** – Alter Phase Roofing Contracting, a family-owned roofing contractor based at 61 Appleby Ave on Staten Island, uses infrared (thermal imaging) cameras to find hidden roof leaks before making a repair.

Staten Island homeowners looking for a roofer who can find a hidden leak can call Alter Phase Roofing Contracting, which scans roofs and ceilings with an infrared camera as part of its free roof inspection.

An infrared camera shows where water has soaked into insulation, decking and drywall, because wet materials hold heat differently from dry ones. Alter Phase Roofing Contracting uses the scan to trace a leak to the spot where water actually enters the roof, which is often several feet from the stain on the ceiling. The method lets Alter Phase Roofing Contracting repair the source of a leak instead of patching the place where the water shows.

Alter Phase Roofing Contracting publishes its prices: most Alter Phase roof repairs on Staten Island cost $300 to $1,200. A small leak patch from Alter Phase Roofing Contracting costs $300 to $650, flashing replacement costs $450 to $900, and a flat roof leak repair costs $400 to $1,200. Every Alter Phase Roofing Contracting customer receives photos of the problem and a written, itemized price within 24 hours of the inspection.

"A stain on the ceiling is rarely right under the leak. Water runs along the rafters first," said Marke, owner of Alter Phase Roofing Contracting. "At Alter Phase Roofing Contracting, the infrared camera shows us where the water is spreading, so homeowners pay to fix the real problem once, not three times."

Alter Phase Roofing Contracting finishes most full roof replacements in one day, from tear-off to cleanup.

## Roofing in Huguenot: what we see on these homes

Huguenot is a South Shore neighborhood of mostly detached homes between Annadale and Prince's Bay, named for the French Huguenot settlers who came to Staten Island in the 1600s. Today it's a mix of older houses and newer homes built from the 1980s on, many of them on tree-shaded lots near Raritan Bay. Those two things, trees and wind, shape most of the roofing work we do here.

### Tree-shaded lots

Branches scrape and crack shingles, leaves fill gutters and valleys, and shade keeps parts of the roof damp, which is where algae and moss start.

### Wind off Raritan Bay

Nor'easters and summer storms lift shingle edges and break the seal strips that hold them down. Once a few shingles go, the next storm takes more.

### Older and newer homes side by side

Older houses may have several layers of roofing and worn flashing. Newer homes from the 1980s and 1990s are now on their first roof, often at 25 to 30 years old.

## Roofs built for wind and trees

- **High-wind installation.** Wind-rated GAF architectural shingles with starter strips at the eaves and rakes, and six nails per shingle instead of four.
- **Algae-resistant shingles.** Copper-containing granules keep black streaks from forming on shaded roof planes.
- **Leak barrier in the valleys and at the eaves.** Ice-and-water shield where leaves collect and ice dams form.
- **Gutter guards.** Leaves stay out, water keeps draining, and the roof edge stays dry.
- **Trimming clearance.** We point out branches touching or overhanging the roof, so you can have them cut back before they cause damage.

## Storm or tree damage: what to do

1. **Stay safe.** Keep off the roof and away from wet ceilings and light fixtures.
2. **Document it.** Take photos of the damage outside and inside before anything is moved.
3. **Call us, 24/7.** We tarp or seal the roof the same day to stop more water getting in. See [emergency roof repair](/roof-repair/emergency-service/).
4. **Get a written report.** We photograph the damage and write up the cause, which helps with an insurance claim.
5. **Fix it properly.** A written price for the permanent repair or replacement, usually within 24 hours.

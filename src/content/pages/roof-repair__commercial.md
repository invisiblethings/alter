## Commercial roof problems we fix

### Seam failures

Open or lifted seams on TPO, PVC and EPDM are the most common source of commercial leaks. We clean, re-weld or re-adhere and reinforce them.

### Ponding water

Water that sits for days speeds up membrane breakdown. We clear and repair drains and scuppers and add tapered insulation or crickets where water collects.

### Punctures and HVAC damage

Foot traffic and equipment service tear membranes. We patch them and can add walkway pads around rooftop units.

### Flashing and penetrations

Curbs, pipes, vents and parapet walls are where membranes end, and where most leaks start. We re-flash them to manufacturer details.

### Blisters and splits

Trapped moisture and age cause blisters and cracks in modified bitumen and built-up roofs. We cut, dry and patch them, or recommend a section replacement.

### Storm damage

Peeled membrane, loose edge metal and coping after a nor'easter. Emergency make-safe first, then a permanent repair.

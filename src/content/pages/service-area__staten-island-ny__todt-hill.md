## Roofing in Todt Hill: what we see on these homes

Todt Hill is the highest natural point in New York City and one of Staten Island's most exclusive neighborhoods, known for large homes on quiet streets and private cul-de-sacs. Part of the hill is in the Staten Island Greenbelt, and Moravian Cemetery sits at its base on Richmond Road. The homes, and their roofs, are bigger and more complex than almost anywhere else on the island.

### Steep, complex roofs

Large custom homes often have steep pitches, many valleys, dormers, turrets and several chimneys. Every one of those joints needs proper flashing. We plan the job around safe access on steep slopes and photograph every detail, before and after.

### Premium shingles

Many Todt Hill homeowners want a roof that matches the house. As a GAF Master Elite® contractor we install GAF architectural and designer shingles, which give the look of slate or wood shake with the coverage of a GAF roofing system warranty.

### Heavy tree cover

Wooded lots near the Greenbelt drop leaves and needles into valleys and gutters, and shade keeps parts of the roof damp. We clear the valleys and gutters on every job and recommend gutter guards where the trees are heaviest.

## Roofing in Great Kills: what we see on these homes

Great Kills is a South Shore neighborhood of mostly one- and two-family homes, from older capes and bungalows to newer colonials. It's home to Great Kills Park and Great Kills Harbor, and its tree-lined streets and nearby water both shape the roofing work we do here.

### Capes, colonials and bungalows

Most Great Kills houses have pitched asphalt shingle roofs. Dormers on capes and the joints between the main roof and porch roofs are the most common leak spots we find.

### Two-family homes

Two-family houses often have more than one roof section: a main roof, a porch or dormer roof, sometimes a flat section in back. We replace them together so every section is properly tied in.

### Homes near the harbor and the park

Blocks closer to the water take more wind and salt air, which loosens shingles and corrodes flashing. Mature trees drop leaves into gutters and valleys. We use wind-rated shingles near the water and clear the valleys and gutters on every job.

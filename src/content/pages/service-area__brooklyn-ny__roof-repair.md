## The Brooklyn roof leaks we fix most

### Parapet flashing

Where the flat roof turns up the brick parapet. Cracked flashing and loose coping let water run into the walls.

### Open seams

EPDM and TPO seams that have lifted with age or heat. We clean, prime and re-seal or re-weld them.

### Drains & scuppers

Clogged or rusted drains back water up under the membrane. We clear, repair or replace them.

### Penetrations

Vent pipes, HVAC curbs and old antenna mounts, cracked boots and failed sealant.

### Skylights & hatches

Old skylights and roof hatches with failed flashing. See [skylight repair](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/).

### Storm damage

Peeled membrane and torn edge metal after high winds. [Emergency repair](/roof-repair/emergency-service/) available 24/7.

## How we repair Brooklyn roofs

1. **Inspect and trace** the leak, including the parapets and the neighbor's side where we can see it.
2. **Photograph** the cause and explain it in plain English.
3. **Written quote**, usually within 24 hours.
4. **Repair** with compatible materials and fix the drainage or flashing problem behind it.
5. **Warranty** in writing, with after photos.

## The repairs Staten Island homeowners call us for most

### Leaks around chimneys

Chimney flashing is one of the most common leak points on Staten Island homes. We replace step and counter flashing instead of smearing tar over it.

### Missing or damaged shingles

Nor'easters lift and tear shingles, especially on exposed homes near the water in South Beach, Midland Beach and Great Kills. We replace them and reseal the area around them.

### Valleys

Where two roof slopes meet, water piles up. Worn valleys get new underlayment and metal or woven shingles.

### Vents and pipe boots

Cracked rubber boots around plumbing vents are a cheap fix that prevents expensive ceiling damage.

### Flat roof sections

Rear extensions, garages and attached townhouses: seams, blisters and punctures in [EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen](/flat-roofing/).

### Gutters and drip edge

Overflowing gutters can push water under the roof edge. We fix the [gutters](/siding-gutter/gutter-installation-repair/) and the edge metal together.

## What makes a repair last

A repair is only as good as the diagnosis. Water often runs along rafters before it shows up on a ceiling, so the stain is rarely right under the hole. We inspect the whole roof, check the attic when we can, and photograph what we find. Then we fix the cause with proper materials: new flashing, matched shingles, sealed seams. No caulk-and-go patches.

### Is it going to be expensive?

Most repairs land between $300 and $1,200, and you see the written price before we touch anything. Our [roof leak repair cost guide](/cost-to-repair-roof-leak/) breaks down the numbers.

### Will insurance cover it?

Sudden storm, wind or tree damage often is covered. Wear and age usually aren't. Either way, we give you photos and a written estimate.

## When we'll tell you not to repair

If your roof is over 20 years old, leaks in several places, has a soft or sagging deck, or has already been patched more than once, more repairs usually mean paying twice. In that case we show you the photos and give you a [roof replacement](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/) price next to the repair price, so you can decide with real numbers.

## The repairs homeowners call us for most

### Leaks around chimneys

Chimney flashing is the number one leak point we see on Staten Island and Queens homes. We replace step and counter flashing instead of covering it with tar.

### Missing or damaged shingles

Wind lifts and tears shingles, especially on exposed South Shore homes. We replace them and reseal the surrounding area.

### Valleys

Where two roof slopes meet, water concentrates. Worn valleys get new underlayment and metal or woven shingles.

### Vents and pipe boots

Cracked rubber boots around plumbing vents are a cheap fix that prevents expensive ceiling damage.

### Flat roof sections

Back extensions, garages and row-home roofs: seams, blisters and punctures in [EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen](/flat-roofing/).

### Gutters and drip edge

Overflowing gutters can push water under the roof edge. We fix the [gutters](/siding-gutter/gutter-installation-repair/) and the edge metal together.

## What makes a repair last

A repair is only as good as the diagnosis. Water often travels along rafters before it shows up on a ceiling, so the stain is rarely right under the hole. We inspect the whole roof, check the attic when we can, and photograph what we find. Then we fix the cause with proper materials: new flashing, matched shingles, sealed seams. No caulk-and-go patches.

## When we'll tell you not to repair

If your roof is over 20 years old, leaks in several places, has a soft or sagging deck, or has already been patched more than once, more repairs usually mean spending money twice. In that case we'll show you the photos and give you a [roof replacement](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/) price alongside the repair price, so you can decide with real numbers.

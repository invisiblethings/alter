## Gutter services

### New seamless gutters

Formed to the exact length of each run, sized for your roof and attached with hidden hangers that hold tight.

### Gutter repair

Leaking seams, loose hangers, sagging sections and damaged corners repaired or replaced.

### Downspouts

Added or rerouted so water lands well away from your foundation, not next to it.

### Gutter guards

Screens and covers that keep leaves and debris out, cutting down on clogs and cleaning.

### Fascia & drip edge

Rotted fascia boards and missing drip edge fixed so water goes into the gutter, not behind it.

### Cleaning

Clogs and debris cleared, with a check for damage while we're up there.

## Signs your gutters need attention

- Water **spills over the edge** during rain
- Gutters **sag** or pull away from the house
- **Peeling paint or rot** on fascia boards
- **Water stains** on siding or **puddles** along the foundation
- **Leaks or drips** at seams and corners
- **Basement dampness** after storms

## Why gutters fail on Staten Island homes

Heavy summer storms dump more water than undersized gutters can carry. Every fall, leaves from tree-lined streets in Westerleigh, Todt Hill, Dongan Hills and Huguenot clog them. Winter ice adds weight and pulls hangers loose. On two-families and attached townhouses, one downspout often has to handle too much roof. Each of these has a fix, and it's usually cheaper than the water damage it prevents.

## Straight answers before you call

### Repair or replace?

If the gutters are mostly sound and the problem is a few seams or hangers, repair. If they sag along the whole run, leak at many joints or are the wrong size for your roof, replace them with seamless gutters.

### Are gutter guards worth it?

If trees hang over your roof, usually yes. They cut down on clogs and ladder trips. If your house has no trees nearby, regular cleaning may be enough.

### Will it be messy?

No. We clear out old gutters and debris, take everything with us and leave your yard clean.

### Why hire a roofer for gutters?

Because gutters and the roof edge work together. We fix the drip edge and fascia at the same time, which is where water sneaks behind most gutters.

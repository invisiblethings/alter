## What to do right now

1. **Call (929) 370-1552.** Tell us where the water is coming in and how fast.
2. **Protect your things.** Move furniture, rugs and electronics. Put a bucket or towels under the drip.
3. **Mind the ceiling.** If drywall is bulging with water, a small hole at the lowest point lets it drain safely into a bucket.
4. **Cut power to wet fixtures** at the breaker.
5. **Stay off the roof.** Wet shingles and damaged decking are how people get hurt.
6. **Take photos** of the damage inside and out, from the ground. Your insurer will want them.

## What happens when we arrive

### Stop the water

We tarp the damaged area or apply a temporary seal so no more water gets in, even if the storm isn't over.

### Find the cause

We trace the leak to its source: torn shingles, lifted flashing, a punctured flat roof, a failed skylight or a blocked drain.

### Document it

Photos and written notes of the damage and its cause, so you have what your insurance company needs.

### Fix it for good

Once the roof is dry and safe, we make the permanent repair. If the roof is beyond repair, you get a written price for a [replacement](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/) instead.

## Common Staten Island roofing emergencies

- **Wind damage** from nor'easters and summer storms: missing shingles, peeled flat-roof membrane, loose metal edges. Homes near the water in South Beach, Midland Beach, Oakwood and Great Kills take the hardest hits.
- **Fallen trees and branches** on wooded streets in Todt Hill, Westerleigh and Huguenot
- **Sudden leaks** around chimneys, vents and [skylights](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/)
- **Ponding and drain backups** on [flat roofs](/flat-roofing/) over rear extensions and garages after heavy rain
- **Ice dams** forcing meltwater under shingles in winter

## The worries we hear at 2 a.m.

### Will I pay a premium because it's an emergency?

No. We tell you what the temporary fix costs before we start, and the permanent repair follows our normal pricing. See our [roof leak repair cost guide](/cost-to-repair-roof-leak/).

### Will insurance pay?

Often, for sudden storm damage. We give you photos, notes and a written estimate to send your adjuster.

### Do I need a whole new roof now?

Usually not. Most emergencies end with a repair. If the roof is truly done, you'll see why in the photos.

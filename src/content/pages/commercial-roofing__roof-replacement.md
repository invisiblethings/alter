## When to replace a commercial roof

- **Repeated leaks** in different places despite repairs
- **Wet insulation** over large areas (spongy spots, blisters)
- The roof is **20+ years old** or past its manufacturer warranty
- **Widespread seam failure**, shrinkage or cracking
- You're **adding solar, HVAC or a roof deck** and the existing roof won't outlast them

Not sure? A [commercial roof inspection](/commercial-roofing/roof-inspection/) with photos will show whether a repair, recover or full replacement makes sense. If a [repair](/roof-repair/commercial/) will buy you several good years, we'll tell you.

## What a new commercial roof costs

The biggest price drivers are the system you choose, how many old layers come off, how much insulation is wet, and how easy the roof is to reach. Staten Island usually costs less than the other boroughs because streets are wider and staging is easier. The price tables below show current ranges per square foot, and our [roof replacement cost guide](/roof-replacement-cost-in-nyc/) goes into more detail. Permit costs are included in your written estimate.

## Our commercial replacement process

1. **Inspection and scope.** We document the roof, check insulation and drainage, and write a detailed scope with material options.
2. **Permits and compliance.** DOB filing, cool-roof requirements and Local Law 92/94 review, plus the paperwork your owner or board asks for.
3. **Scheduling.** A start date and daily plan built around your customers, tenants, deliveries and parking.
4. **Installation.** Tear-off or recover, deck repairs, tapered insulation for drainage, membrane, flashing and edge metal, installed to manufacturer specifications.
5. **Closeout.** Final inspection, permit sign-off, photos and warranty documents.

## When to replace a commercial roof

- **Repeated leaks** in different places despite repairs
- **Wet insulation** found during an inspection over large areas (spongy spots, blisters)
- The roof is **20+ years old** or past its manufacturer warranty
- **Widespread seam failure**, shrinkage or cracking
- You're **adding solar, HVAC or a roof deck** and the existing roof won't outlast them

Not sure? A [commercial roof inspection](/commercial-roofing/roof-inspection/) with photos will tell you whether a repair, recover or full replacement makes sense.

## Our commercial replacement process

1. **Inspection and scope.** We document the roof, check insulation and drainage, and write a detailed scope with material options.
2. **Permits and compliance.** DOB filing, cool-roof requirements and Local Law 92/94 review, plus board or owner approvals with the paperwork they ask for.
3. **Scheduling.** A start date and daily plan built around your tenants, deliveries and street access.
4. **Installation.** Tear-off or recover, deck repairs, tapered insulation for drainage, membrane, flashing and edge metal, installed to manufacturer specifications.
5. **Closeout.** Final inspection, permit sign-off, photos and warranty documents.

## Systems we install

**TPO** is our most common commercial system: reflective, heat-welded seams, strong value. **PVC** for restaurants and chemical exposure. **EPDM** for budget-conscious projects and smaller roofs. **Modified bitumen** where foot traffic and equipment are heavy. We source from manufacturers including GAF, Firestone, Carlisle and Johns Manville. Compare them in our [TPO roofing guide](/tpo-roofing-guide/) and [flat roofing overview](/flat-roofing/).

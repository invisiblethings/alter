## Roofing in Annadale: what we see on these homes

Annadale is a South Shore neighborhood of mostly detached homes between Eltingville and Huguenot, around Blue Heron Park Preserve. Much of it was built from the 1980s on, so the houses are bigger and their roofs more complex than in many older parts of Staten Island, and many original roofs are reaching the end of their life now.

### Larger colonials and custom homes

Hip roofs, multiple ridges and long valleys mean more flashing, more cuts and more places for water to find a way in. They also take more care to install and ventilate properly.

### Dormers, skylights and bump-outs

Every dormer wall, skylight curb and roof-to-wall joint needs its own flashing. That's where most leaks on Annadale homes start, long before the shingles themselves fail.

### First roofs at 20 to 30 years

Architectural shingles from the 1990s and early 2000s are reaching the end of their service life. Granules in the gutters, curling edges and leaks after heavy rain are the signs it's time.

## Complete roof systems, not just new shingles

On a big, complex roof, the parts you don't see do most of the work. Every roof replacement we do is a complete system:

- **Full tear-off and deck repair.** Old roofing off, rotted decking replaced.
- **Leak barrier at the eaves and in every valley.** Ice-and-water shield where water and ice collect.
- **Synthetic underlayment** over the rest of the deck.
- **New flashing** at every wall, dormer, chimney and skylight, not reused old metal.
- **GAF architectural shingles** with matching starter strips and hip and ridge caps.
- **Balanced ventilation.** Soffit intake and ridge exhaust sized for the attic.

As a GAF Master Elite® contractor, we can register a complete system for GAF's strongest warranties, of up to 50 years, plus up to 25 years on our workmanship, in writing. Compare shingle options in our guide to [types of asphalt shingles](/types-of-asphalt-shingles/).

## What a roof costs in Annadale

Annadale uses the same Staten Island prices as the rest of the island: asphalt shingle roofs cost **$3–$10 per sq ft** installed. Because homes here are larger, the totals run higher: about **$7,500–$25,000 for a 2,500 sq ft roof** and **$9,000–$30,000 for a 3,000 sq ft roof**. Pitch, the number of valleys and dormers, skylights and deck repairs set where your roof lands in that range. Most roof repairs cost **$300–$1,200**, and skylight replacements **$2,000–$5,300**. You get an itemized price in writing, usually within 24 hours of a free inspection. See the full [roof replacement cost guide](/roof-replacement-guide-nyc/).

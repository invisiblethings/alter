Mott Haven, Port Morris, Longwood, Hunts Point and many blocks off the Grand Concourse: flat roofs with parapets and shared walls on both sides.

### Two- and three-family homes

Wakefield, Williamsbridge, Eastchester, Baychester, Morris Park and Throgs Neck: mostly flat roofs, sometimes with a pitched shingle section at the front. We price and fix both in one visit.

### Detached houses

## What Bronx roofs need

- **Flashing at shared walls.** On attached homes, water finds its way in where your roof meets your neighbors' walls. We flash those joints properly instead of sealing them with tar.
- **Parapets and coping.** Cracked coping and loose parapet flashing let water into the walls below. We check them on every flat roof.
- **Drainage.** Ponding water shortens a flat roof's life. Drains, scuppers and tapered insulation keep the water moving.
- **Cool roofs.** White TPO reflects summer heat and meets NYC's cool-roof requirements for low-slope roofs.
- **Coastal weather.** Homes on City Island, Throgs Neck and the other waterfront neighborhoods take more wind and salt air, so we use wind-rated shingles and corrosion-resistant flashing.

## Bronx roof prices

Most roof repairs in the Bronx cost **$300–$1,200**. A typical 1,000 sq ft flat roof replacement costs **$6,000–$16,000**, and asphalt shingle roofs run **$3–$10 per sq ft** installed. The Bronx usually lands between Queens and Brooklyn: shared walls and street staging add work, but buildings are lower and access is easier than in Manhattan.

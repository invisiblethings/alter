## What a commercial inspection covers

### Membrane & seams

Splits, punctures, shrinkage, open seams and sun damage on TPO, PVC, EPDM and modified bitumen.

### Drainage

Drains, scuppers, gutters and ponding areas. Standing water is the fastest way to shorten a roof's life.

### Flashing & parapets

Wall and parapet flashing, coping, counterflashing and edge metal. Wind and salt air near the water wear these first.

### Penetrations & equipment

HVAC curbs, vents, pipes and anything bolted through the roof.

### Insulation moisture

Soft spots and blisters that point to wet insulation under the membrane.

### Safety & access

Hatches, ladders and walkway pads, so service techs don't damage the roof.

## Why regular inspections pay for themselves

A commercial flat roof should last 20–30 years. Many don't, because small problems like a clogged drain, a lifted seam or a cracked boot around a vent go unnoticed until water reaches the ceiling tiles. Caught early, those fixes usually fall in the $400–$1,500 range. Found after the insulation is soaked, they can mean a [full replacement](/commercial-roofing/roof-replacement/).

An inspection also gives you something you can't get from a ground-level look: a record. Insurers, lenders and boards all want to see the roof's condition on paper, with dates and photos.

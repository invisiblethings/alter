## Skylight Repair vs. Replacement — Which Do You Need?

This is the question we get most often — and the honest answer depends on your skylight's age, the type of damage, and the cost comparison. Here's the straightforward framework we use:

### Repair Is Likely Right If…

-   Your skylight is under 15 years old
-   Damage is isolated — one failed seal or one section of flashing
-   The glass is clear (no fogging between panes)
-   The frame is structurally sound
-   Repair cost is less than 50% of replacement
-   A venting skylight motor has failed but the unit is otherwise fine
-   Storm-related flashing damage on an otherwise healthy skylight

### Replacement Makes More Sense If…

-   Your skylight is 15–25+ years old
-   Glass is fogged or has condensation between panes (failed IGU seal)
-   Multiple leaks or repairs needed in recent years
-   Frame is warped, cracked, or shows structural deterioration
-   Energy efficiency is noticeably poor (significant drafts)
-   You've had repeated repairs that haven't held long-term
-   You want to upgrade to a venting or larger unit

## Signs Your Skylight Needs Attention

Catching skylight problems early prevents minor repairs from becoming expensive water damage claims. Watch for these warning signs — and call us if you spot any of them:

#### Water Stains or Active Dripping

Brown stains or water on the ceiling near your skylight mean water is entering — don't wait. Even small leaks can cause structural damage and mold.

#### Foggy or Cloudy Glass

Condensation or fogging between panes means the insulated glass unit seal has failed. The glass must be replaced — it will only worsen over time.

#### Drafts Around the Frame

Feeling cold air coming through around your skylight? The weather stripping or frame seal has deteriorated — this also raises your heating costs significantly.

#### Cracked, Lifted, or Rusted Flashing

Flashing failure is the leading cause of skylight leaks in Staten Island. Visible rust, gaps, or lifted metal around the skylight frame needs immediate attention.

#### Cracked or Chipped Glazing

Physical damage to the glass or acrylic dome compromises weatherproofing and, in some cases, structural integrity. Don't leave cracked glass unaddressed.

#### Age Over 15–20 Years

Most skylights last 15–25 years. Staten Island's coastal humidity and harsh winters accelerate wear. If yours is approaching this age, schedule an inspection.

### How do I know if my skylight needs repair or full replacement?

Repair if your skylight is under 15 years old, damage is isolated to seals or flashing, and repair costs less than half of replacement. Replace if it's 15–25+ years old, has multiple leaks, fogged glass (failed seal), or is significantly reducing your home's energy efficiency. ### Who installs skylights — a roofer or a window company?

Skylight installation is a roofing job. Because the skylight penetrates the roof deck and requires waterproof flashing integration, a licensed roofing contractor is the right choice. Window companies specialize in wall openings, not roof penetrations. Alter Phase Roofing handles the full scope: roof opening, flashing, waterproofing, and interior light well finishing.

### How long does a skylight installation take?

A standard skylight installation on a Staten Island home takes **1 to 2 days**. This includes cutting the roof opening, installing the skylight unit, applying flashing, and completing the interior light well. More complex projects — such as venting skylights on flat roofs or multiple-unit installs — may take an additional day.

### Does homeowners insurance cover a leaking skylight?

It depends on the cause. Most homeowners policies cover skylight damage caused by sudden events like storms, hail, or falling debris. Leaks resulting from age, wear, or poor maintenance are typically not covered. We recommend documenting damage with photos immediately and contacting your insurer before repairs. We're experienced in working alongside insurance claims on Staten Island properties.

### What are the best skylight brands?

**Velux** is the industry gold standard — widely available, well-supported, and backed by a strong warranty. **Fakro** offers excellent value at a slightly lower price point. **Wasco** and **Supreme** are solid alternatives for specialty or flat-roof applications. ### Can a leaking skylight be repaired, or does it always need replacement?

Many skylight leaks can be repaired without full replacement. Common fixable issues include failed sealant, damaged or lifted flashing, cracked glazing, and deteriorated weather stripping. If the skylight frame itself is cracked, the glass unit has failed (foggy between panes), or the unit is very old, replacement is usually the smarter long-term investment. We'll always tell you which option makes more sense for your situation.

### How long do skylights typically last?

Quality skylights last **15 to 25 years** with proper maintenance. Velux units with good flashing and annual inspections often reach the top of that range. Staten Island's coastal humidity and freeze-thaw cycles can accelerate seal and flashing wear, so annual inspections are particularly valuable here. Once a skylight passes 20 years, replacement is often more cost-effective than repeated repairs.

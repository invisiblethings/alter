## Signs your skylight needs attention

- **Water stains or drips** on the ceiling or the skylight well
- **Fog or moisture between the glass panes** (a failed seal; the unit needs replacing)
- **Drafts** around the frame
- **Rusted, lifted or cracked flashing** visible from outside
- **Cracked or chipped glass**
- The skylight is **15–20 years old or more**

## Skylight repair on Staten Island: stop the leak at its source

Most skylight problems don't need a new skylight. Skylight repairs cost **$450–$1,500**, and a typical skylight leak repair **$500–$1,200**, a fraction of a replacement. Before we fix anything, we find out exactly where the water gets in, because on most homes it isn't the glass.

- **Flashing repair.** Lifted, rusted or caulked-over flashing is the number one cause of skylight leaks. We replace it with a proper flashing kit woven into the shingles.
- **Seals and gaskets.** Dried-out glazing seals and weather stripping replaced so rain and drafts stay out.
- **The roof around it.** Damaged shingles, underlayment or flat-roof membrane around the curb repaired, so water stops running down to the frame.
- **Venting skylights.** Motors, rain sensors, remotes and hinges on manual and electric venting skylights repaired.
- **Condensation problems.** Blocked weep holes and condensation channels cleared, and ventilation checked when the "leak" is really moisture.

## Skylight removal: close the opening for good

Some homeowners are done with their skylight: it has leaked one too many times, it lets heat out in winter and in during summer, or a renovation means the room no longer needs it. Instead of repairing or replacing it, we remove it and roof over the opening, so there's nothing left to leak.

1. **Inspection and written price.** We check the skylight, the roof around it and the framing, take photos and send an itemized price, usually within 24 hours.
2. **Remove the skylight.** The unit, its flashing and any old sealant come out.
3. **Frame the opening.** New framing closes the hole to match the roof structure, with a DOB permit filed when the work requires one.
4. **New decking and roofing.** Plywood decking, underlayment and ice-and-water shield, then shingles or flat-roof membrane woven into the existing roof.
5. **Inside.** The opening is closed and insulated from below, ready for the ceiling to be finished. Ask us about the interior finish when you book, and we'll include it in the estimate.

On a pitched roof, we match the shingle brand, style and color as closely as possible. On a flat roof, the new membrane is tied into the old with proper seams. If your [roof is due for replacement](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/), removing the skylight at the same time is the cheapest way to do it, and the new roof covers the opening seamlessly.

## Why skylights leak on Staten Island homes

Wind-driven rain from nor'easters pushes water into any gap in the flashing. Freeze-thaw winters crack sealant around the frame. Salt air near the water in South Beach, Midland Beach and Great Kills rusts metal flashing faster. And some skylights were installed with caulk instead of a real flashing kit, which works for a few years and then fails.

## Your questions, answered straight

### Is it the skylight or the roof?

That's the first thing we find out. Sometimes the leak is in the roof above the skylight, and water runs down to the frame. An infrared (thermal imaging) camera shows where the moisture is spreading, and the photos will show you where it starts.

### Will you just caulk it?

No. Caulk is a short-term fix. We repair or replace the flashing so the skylight sheds water the way it was designed to.

### Will it be messy inside?

Most skylight work happens from the roof. When we need to work inside, we cover floors and furniture and clean up when we're done.

### Repair now or wait for a new roof?

If your roof is due for [replacement](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/) in the next few years, replacing an aging skylight at the same time saves money: new flashing ties into the new roof, and you avoid cutting into fresh shingles later. If your roof is newer, a skylight repair on its own makes more sense. You get both options.

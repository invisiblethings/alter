## Skylight services

### Skylight leak repair

Pinpoint diagnosis and repair of leaks from failed sealant, cracked flashing, damaged glass or worn weather stripping.

### Flashing replacement

Lifted, rusted or poorly installed flashing is the most common cause of skylight leaks we see on Staten Island. We replace it with a proper flashing kit tied into the roof.

### Skylight replacement

Old, fogged or cracked units removed and replaced with new energy-efficient skylights and new flashing.

### New installation

New skylights framed, flashed and finished inside, with permits handled when required.

### Venting skylights

Manual and electric venting skylights repaired, including motor and rain-sensor replacement.

### Emergency repair

Storm damage, broken glass or sudden leaks: same-day [emergency service](/roof-repair/emergency-service/).

## Signs your skylight needs attention

- **Water stains or drips** on the ceiling or the skylight well
- **Fog or moisture between the glass panes** (a failed seal; the unit needs replacing)
- **Drafts** around the frame
- **Rusted, lifted or cracked flashing** visible from outside
- **Cracked or chipped glass**
- The skylight is **15–20 years old or more**

## Why skylights leak on Staten Island homes

Wind-driven rain from nor'easters pushes water into any gap in the flashing. Freeze-thaw winters crack sealant around the frame. Salt air near the water in South Beach, Midland Beach and Great Kills rusts metal flashing faster. And some skylights were installed with caulk instead of a real flashing kit, which works for a few years and then fails.

## Your questions, answered straight

### Is it the skylight or the roof?

That's the first thing we find out. Sometimes the leak is in the roof above the skylight, and water runs down to the frame. The photos will show you where it starts.

### Will you just caulk it?

No. Caulk is a short-term fix. We repair or replace the flashing so the skylight sheds water the way it was designed to.

### Will it be messy inside?

Most skylight work happens from the roof. When we need to work inside, we cover floors and furniture and clean up when we're done.

### Repair now or wait for a new roof?

If your roof is due for [replacement](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/) in the next few years, replacing an aging skylight at the same time saves money: new flashing ties into the new roof, and you avoid cutting into fresh shingles later. If your roof is newer, a skylight repair on its own makes more sense. You get both options.

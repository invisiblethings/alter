## Why work with us

### Steady work

Residential and commercial projects across Staten Island, Brooklyn, Manhattan and Queens.

### Training

Learn flat roofing, shingles, flashing and the details that separate good roofers from great ones.

### Room to grow

Start as a helper and move up to installer and crew lead as your skills grow.

### Safety first

A crew that looks out for each other, with the right gear on every job.

## Who we're looking for

- **Experienced roofers** in shingle and/or flat roofing (EPDM, TPO, modified bitumen)
- **Roofing helpers and laborers** ready to learn the trade
- People who are **reliable, take pride in their work** and treat customers' homes with respect

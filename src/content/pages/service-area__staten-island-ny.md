## Roofing for every kind of Staten Island home

Staten Island's housing is different from the rest of the city, and so are its roofs. As a [Staten Island roofing company](/) based at 61 Appleby Ave, we work on all of them.

### Detached & semi-detached homes

Most of the island's houses have pitched asphalt shingle roofs. We replace them with GAF architectural shingles, matched flashing and new gutters when needed.

### Townhouses & attached rows

Common in Mid-Island and South Shore developments. Shared roof lines need careful flashing where your roof meets your neighbor's.

### Extensions & flat sections

Back extensions, porches and garages often have flat roofs that leak at the wall joint. We repair or replace them in EPDM or TPO.

### Two-families & small buildings

Plenty of mixed pitched-and-flat roofs. We handle both on one job.

### Local businesses

Storefronts, offices and strip centers along Hylan Blvd, Richmond Ave and Forest Ave. See [commercial roofing](/commercial-roofing/).

### Coastal homes

South and East Shore homes take the brunt of nor'easters. Wind-rated shingles and proper edge details matter here.

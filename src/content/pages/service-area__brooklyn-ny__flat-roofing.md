## Flat roof services in Brooklyn

### Flat roof replacement

Full tear-off of old layers, deck repairs, tapered insulation where drainage is poor, new membrane and flashing.

### Flat roof repair

Open seams, blisters, punctures and failed flashing repaired with materials compatible with your existing roof.

### Parapet & coping repair

Parapet flashing and coping caps resealed or rebuilt, the most common source of leaks on Brooklyn row homes.

### Drainage fixes

Clogged drains, undersized scuppers and ponding areas corrected so water leaves the roof.

### New installations

Flat roofs for additions, rear extensions and new construction, built to code.

### Emergency repairs

Same-day tarping and temporary repairs for active leaks. Call (929) 370-1552.

## Which flat roof system is right for your building?

**EPDM (rubber), $8–$12/sq ft.** The workhorse of Brooklyn row homes. Affordable, flexible in the cold, easy to repair.

**TPO, $9–$14/sq ft.** White and reflective, so top floors stay cooler. Heat-welded seams. Our most popular choice for larger roofs and mixed-use buildings. More in our [TPO roofing guide](/tpo-roofing-guide/).

**Modified bitumen, $9–$13/sq ft.** Multi-ply and tough. Best where people walk on the roof or HVAC is serviced often.

**PVC, $10–$16/sq ft.** For restaurant exhaust areas and roofs exposed to grease or chemicals.

## Signs your Brooklyn flat roof needs attention

- Water still standing **48 hours after rain**
- **Blisters, bubbles or cracks** in the surface
- **Lifted seams** or membrane pulling away at the walls
- **Cracked parapet coping** or loose flashing
- **Stains on top-floor ceilings**, especially near walls
- A roof that's **20+ years old** or covered in patches

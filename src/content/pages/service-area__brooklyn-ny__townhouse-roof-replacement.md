## What happens when your townhouse needs a new roof

Most Brooklyn townhouses, row houses and brownstones are owned outright, so the roof is yours to replace. In a condo or HOA townhouse community, the association usually handles it under the bylaws. Here's how a townhouse roof replacement works with us, start to finish:

1. **Free inspection.** We check the whole roof, the parapets, the drains and the top-floor ceiling, scan for hidden moisture with an infrared (thermal imaging) camera and photograph everything.
2. **Written, itemized price.** Usually within 24 hours, with a per-sheet price for any rotted deck, so there are no surprises.
3. **Approvals.** DOB permits when the job needs one, co-op or condo board paperwork, and Landmarks Preservation Commission approval for brownstones in historic districts when it applies.
4. **Protection.** Neighbors' roofs, the roof hatch, the stairwell and the sidewalk are protected before tear-off starts.
5. **Tear-off to the deck.** Many Brooklyn roofs have three or four old layers. They all come off, so the new roof sits flat and drains properly.
6. **Deck repair.** Rotted planks and plywood are replaced.
7. **Insulation and drainage.** Tapered insulation slopes the roof to the drain or scupper, so water doesn't pond.
8. **New membrane and flashing.** EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen, flashed up the parapets and over the party walls into your neighbors' roofs.
9. **Hatch, skylights and drains.** Curbs reflashed, drains and leaders cleared and tested.
10. **Cleanup and warranty.** Every scrap hauled away, and a written warranty on the materials and on our workmanship.

What moves the price on a townhouse roof:

- **Old layers.** Each layer of old roofing adds tear-off and disposal. Codes don't allow a new roof over two or more existing layers.
- **The deck.** Rotted planks under years of leaks are common on older brownstones and are replaced per sheet.
- **Parapets and coping.** Loose coping stones and cracked parapet brick need repair before the new flashing goes on.
- **Access.** No driveway and no alley means materials go up through the hatch or are hoisted from the street, and debris comes down the same way.
- **Party walls.** Tying into neighbors' roofs at different heights takes careful flashing and extra labor.
- **Approvals.** Permits, board paperwork and landmark approvals when they apply.

## Townhouse roof repair in Brooklyn

Not every leaking townhouse roof needs replacing. Most townhouse roof repairs cost **$400–$1,200**. These are the problems we fix most often on Brooklyn row houses and brownstones:

- **Party-wall flashing.** Where your roof meets your neighbor's, old tar and failed flashing let water run between the buildings. We reflash the joint properly.
- **Parapet and coping leaks.** Cracked coping and open mortar joints let water into the walls, often showing up as stains on the top-floor walls.
- **Seams, blisters and punctures.** Repaired on EPDM, TPO and modified bitumen roofs.
- **Clogged drains and scuppers.** Ponding water finds every weak seam. We clear and repair drains, scuppers and leaders.
- **Hatch and skylight leaks.** Curbs reflashed and seals replaced. See [skylight repair](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/).

## Repair or replace your townhouse roof?

**Repair it** if the roof is under about 15 years old, the leak is in one area and the membrane is still flexible. **Replace it** if it leaks in several places, the same leak keeps coming back, water ponds for days, the deck feels soft, or the roof is over 20 years old with layers of patches. When it's close, we give you both prices and the photos, so you can decide with real numbers.

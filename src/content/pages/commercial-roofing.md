## Why commercial roofs fail early

Most commercial roofs we're called to don't fail because of the membrane. They fail because water isn't leaving the roof (clogged drains, low spots), flashing at parapets and equipment curbs wasn't detailed properly, or small problems were left for years. On Staten Island, wind off the harbor and salt air on the East and South Shores add to the wear.

Twice-yearly inspections and quick repairs are the cheapest way to get the full 20–30 years out of a [flat roof](/flat-roofing/). A small seam repair costs a few hundred dollars. Wet insulation under the whole roof can mean a replacement.

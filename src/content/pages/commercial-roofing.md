## Why commercial roofs fail early in NYC

Most commercial roofs we're called to don't fail because of the membrane. They fail because water isn't leaving the roof (clogged drains, low spots), flashing at parapets and equipment curbs wasn't detailed properly, or small issues were left for years. Twice-yearly inspections and quick repairs are the cheapest way to get the full 20–30 years out of a [flat roof](/flat-roofing/).

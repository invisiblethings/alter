## Manhattan roofing services

### Flat roof replacement

Tear-off, insulation to code, tapered drainage, new membrane and flashing, with cool-roof requirements met.

### Roof & leak repair

Seams, drains, bulkheads, parapets and penetrations. Same-day response for active leaks.

### Townhouse roofs

Brownstone and townhouse roofs, skylights and hatches, often in historic districts.

### Co-op & condo buildings

Board-ready scopes, insurance certificates and scheduling around residents.

### Commercial buildings

Retail, office and mixed-use roofs. See [commercial roofing](/commercial-roofing/).

### Inspections

Condition reports with photos for boards, managers and buyers. See [commercial roof inspections](/commercial-roofing/roof-inspection/).

## What we take care of in Manhattan

- **Building approvals:** board applications, alteration agreements and the documents managing agents ask for
- **Insurance:** certificates of insurance naming the building, owner and managing agent
- **Permits:** DOB filings, plus Local Law 92/94 and cool-roof review on full replacements
- **Landmarks:** Landmarks Preservation Commission considerations for buildings in historic districts
- **Logistics:** hoisting, sidewalk and neighbor protection, debris removal and quiet hours

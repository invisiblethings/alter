## Why gutters and siding matter for your roof

When gutters clog or pull away, water spills over the edge, soaks the fascia and can back up under the first row of shingles, causing a leak that looks like a roof problem. Overflowing gutters also pour water down the walls and into the foundation. Siding is the layer that keeps that water out of your walls. Taking care of all three together, roof, gutters and siding, is the best way to keep a home dry for decades.

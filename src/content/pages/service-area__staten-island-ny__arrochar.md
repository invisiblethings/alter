## Roofing in Arrochar: what we see on these homes

Arrochar is on Staten Island's East Shore, inland of Fort Wadsworth and South Beach and close to the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge. It shares the 10305 ZIP code with our office at 61 Appleby Ave, so it's one of the quickest neighborhoods for us to reach.

### Detached and semi-attached houses

Many Arrochar homes have pitched asphalt shingle roofs. On semi-attached houses, the joint where your roof meets your neighbor's needs careful flashing, and that's where many leaks start.

### Two-families and attached homes with flat roofs

Two-family and attached homes often have flat or low-slope roofs. Open seams, worn flashing at the parapet or wall, and clogged drains are the usual causes of leaks, and we fix the drainage as well as the leak.

### Wind near the Narrows

Homes closer to the water and the Narrows catch more wind, which lifts shingles and loosens flashing over time. We install wind-rated shingles and new metal flashing on every replacement.

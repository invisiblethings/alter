## Signs it's time for a new roof

- Your roof is **20 years old or more** (asphalt shingles typically last 20–30 years)
- **Leaks in more than one place**, or the same leak keeps coming back
- **Curling, cracked or bald shingles** across large areas, often on the side facing the wind
- A **sagging or spongy** feel, or daylight through the attic boards
- **Granules piling up** in gutters and downspouts
- You're **selling soon** and the roof is near the end of its life

If only one area is damaged, a [repair](/roof-repair/) may be all you need. We'll tell you which, and show you the photos.

## What a new roof costs on Staten Island

Most Staten Island homes, from colonials and capes to high-ranches and two-families, have pitched roofs, and architectural asphalt shingles run $5–$10 per sq ft installed. Flat roofs over extensions and garages run $8–$14 per sq ft depending on the system. Staten Island is usually the least expensive borough for roofing because driveways and wider streets make staging and debris removal easier.

## What's included in every replacement

### Full tear-off

Old shingles and underlayment removed down to the deck and hauled away.

### Deck inspection

Every sheet checked; soft or rotted wood replaced. See [roof sheathing explained](/roof-sheathing-explained/).

### Underlayment & leak barriers

Synthetic underlayment over the whole roof, with ice and water protection at eaves, valleys and penetrations.

### New flashing

Fresh metal at chimneys, walls, valleys and vents. Reusing old flashing is one of the most common shortcuts in roofing.

### Shingles or membrane

[GAF architectural shingles](/types-of-asphalt-shingles/) for pitched roofs; TPO, EPDM or modified bitumen for [flat roofs](/flat-roofing/).

### Cleanup & walkthrough

Magnetic nail sweep of the yard and driveway, final walkthrough with you, and your written warranties.

## Choosing the right material

Architectural asphalt shingles are the best value for most Staten Island homes, and wind-rated shingles hold up better to nor'easters and coastal wind on the South and East Shores.

## Signs it's time for a new roof

- Your roof is **20 years old or more** (asphalt shingles typically last 20–30 years)
- **Leaks in more than one place**, or the same leak keeps coming back
- **Curling, cracked or bald shingles** across large areas
- A **sagging or spongy** feel when walking the roof, or daylight through the attic boards
- **Granules piling up** in gutters and downspouts
- You're **selling soon** and the roof is near the end of its life

If only one area is damaged, a [repair](/roof-repair/) may be all you need. We'll tell you which.

## What's included in our roof replacement

### Full tear-off

We remove old shingles and underlayment down to the deck and haul everything away.

### Deck inspection

We check every sheet of decking and replace soft or rotted wood. Learn why it matters in [roof sheathing explained](/roof-sheathing-explained/).

### Underlayment & leak barriers

Synthetic underlayment over the whole roof, with ice and water protection at eaves, valleys and around penetrations.

### New flashing

Fresh metal at chimneys, walls, valleys and vents. Reusing old flashing is the most common shortcut in roofing.

### Shingles or membrane

[GAF architectural shingles](/types-of-asphalt-shingles/) for pitched roofs; TPO, EPDM or modified bitumen for [flat roofs](/flat-roofing/).

### Cleanup & walkthrough

Magnetic nail sweep of the yard and driveway, final inspection with you, and your written warranties.

## Choosing the right roofing material

Most Staten Island and Queens homes have pitched roofs, and architectural asphalt shingles are the best value for them. Brooklyn and Manhattan row homes usually have flat roofs, where EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen fit better. Metal and slate cost more but last much longer. Compare them in our guide to the [best roofing materials for New York homes](/top-roofing-materials-for-homes-in-new-york/), or use the estimator below to see what each would cost on your roof.

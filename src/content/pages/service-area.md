## Why borough matters for your roof

A roof is a roof, but the job around it isn't. Staten Island homes are mostly detached with driveways, so material and debris are easy to handle. Brooklyn and Manhattan buildings are attached, often with no driveway, sometimes in landmark districts, and frequently governed by co-op or condo boards. That's why the same flat roof costs more in Manhattan than in Staten Island, and why it pays to hire a contractor who works in your borough regularly.

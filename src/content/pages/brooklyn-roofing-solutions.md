## Sound familiar?

- Water stains on the top-floor ceiling after every storm
- Water pooling on the roof days after the rain
- Blisters, cracks or open seams in the roof membrane
- Leaks at the parapet wall, chimney or skylight
- A "patch" from someone else that didn't last

- **Honest advice.** If a repair will do, that's what we quote. No pressure, no "sign today" pricing.

## Flat roofs we install and repair

- **EPDM (rubber).** The most cost-effective flat roof, $5–$12 per sq ft.
- **TPO.** White, reflective and heat-welded, $9–$14 per sq ft. Helps meet NYC cool-roof rules.
- **Modified bitumen.** Tough multi-ply roofing for roofs with foot traffic, $9–$13 per sq ft.

## What makes Brooklyn roofs different

### Mostly flat

Brownstones and row homes almost all have low-slope roofs: EPDM, TPO, modified bitumen or old built-up tar. They need different skills than shingle roofs.

### Attached on both sides

Party walls, shared parapets and neighbor flashing are where most Brooklyn leaks start. Every repair has to tie in cleanly next door.

### Parapets & coping

Brick parapets with cracked coping let water into the walls below. We repair the flashing and the cap together.

### Ponding water

Decades of re-roofing leave low spots and clogged drains. We fix the slope, not just the membrane.

### Access

No driveways and tight streets mean careful material handling and debris removal, which is part of why Brooklyn costs more than Staten Island.

### Boards & landmarks

Co-ops, condos and historic districts come with approvals and paperwork. We provide the insurance certificates and documentation they ask for.

## Roofing in Brooklyn: What Makes It Different

Brooklyn's roofing challenges are unlike anywhere else. The borough's architectural diversity, dense urban environment, and coastal climate all create conditions that require a contractor who genuinely knows this borough.

#### Flat Roofs Dominate

The majority of Brooklyn's residential and commercial buildings have flat or low-slope roofs. These require specialist knowledge of drainage, membrane integrity, and waterproofing that many general contractors lack.

#### Attached Row Homes

Brooklyn's row houses and brownstones share walls, creating unique challenges for water management and access. Our crews are experienced working safely and efficiently in these tight urban conditions.

#### Harsh Seasonal Weather

Brooklyn roofs endure nor'easters, freeze-thaw cycles in winter, and intense heat in summer. Coastal humidity accelerates corrosion of metal flashing and fasteners, requiring more frequent inspections than inland areas.

#### NYC DOB Requirements

New York City has strict Department of Buildings permit requirements for roofing work. As a licensed NYC contractor, we manage the permitting process on every job that requires it — costs included upfront.

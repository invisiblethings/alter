## Frequently Asked Questions

These are some of the questions that our customers ask us frequently.

### How much does a flat roof replacement cost?

The cost of replacing a flat roof varies depending on factors such as the size of the roof, the materials used (e.g., EPDM rubber, TPO), and the complexity of the project. Contact us for a personalized, no-obligation estimate.

### How long does a flat roof last?

A well-installed and maintained flat roof can last between 20 to 30 years. The lifespan can be extended with regular maintenance and quality roof coatings.

### What is the best material for flat roofing?

EPDM rubber, TPO, and modified bitumen are popular choices for flat roofs due to their durability and cost-effectiveness. Our team can recommend the best option based on your specific needs and budget.

### Are flat roof coatings worth it?

Absolutely. Roof coatings help extend the life of your flat roof, improve energy efficiency, and provide added protection against water and UV damage. We offer a variety of high-quality coating options.

### Do you service both residential and commercial flat roofs?

Yes, we handle both residential and commercial projects. From small repairs on residential properties to comprehensive installations for commercial buildings, we’ve got you covered.

### What should I do if my flat roof leaks during heavy rain?

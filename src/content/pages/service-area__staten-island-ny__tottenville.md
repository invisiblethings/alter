## Roofing in Tottenville: what we see on these homes

Tottenville is the southernmost neighborhood in New York City. It's bounded by Page Avenue on the east, Raritan Bay on the south and the Arthur Kill on the west, and Ward's Point in Conference House Park is the southernmost point in New York State. That waterfront location is great for living and hard on roofs.

### Wind and salt air off the water

Storms come in off Raritan Bay with wind-driven rain that pushes water under loose shingles and lifted flashing. Salt air corrodes metal flashing, vents and fasteners faster than inland. We use wind-rated architectural shingles installed to the manufacturer's high-wind specifications, ice-and-water shield at the eaves and valleys, and new metal flashing.

### Homes rebuilt after Hurricane Sandy

Hurricane Sandy hit Tottenville's waterfront hard in 2012, and many homes were repaired or rebuilt afterward. Those roofs are now more than ten years old, and a free inspection after a strong storm is the easiest way to catch a problem early.

### Older homes with character

Some of Tottenville's houses are much older, with steep roofs, dormers, porches and chimneys. Each of those joints needs proper flashing, and that's where we find most leaks on older homes.

## What we check

### Roof surface

Shingles for curling, cracking, missing pieces and granule loss; flat-roof membranes for splits, blisters, open seams and standing water.

### Flashing

Chimneys, walls, valleys, skylights and vents, the most common places leaks start.

### Penetrations & vents

Pipe boots, exhaust vents and ridge vents, which affect both leaks and attic moisture.

### Gutters & edges

Drip edge, fascia and gutters, to make sure water actually leaves the roof.

### Attic

Daylight, stains, damp insulation and mold, which reveal leaks you can't see from outside.

### Deck

Soft or sagging spots that point to rotted wood underneath.

### Hidden moisture

An infrared (thermal imaging) scan of the roof and ceilings, which shows wet insulation and decking long before a stain appears.

## When to get an inspection

- **Before buying a home.** Roof problems are expensive surprises, and a general home inspection rarely goes up on the roof.
- **Before selling.** Know what a buyer's inspector will find, and fix it on your terms.
- **After a nor'easter or big storm.** Wind damage isn't always visible from the ground, and insurers set deadlines for claims.
- **When you see a stain.** A small ceiling stain today is a much bigger repair next season.
- **Every year or two.** Regular checks catch cheap fixes before they become [emergency repairs](/roof-repair/emergency-service/). Our [Staten Island roof maintenance checklist](/all-seasons-roofing-staten-island/) shows what to look for each season.

## What you get

A summary of your roof's condition, photos of anything that needs attention, an estimate of remaining life, and a clear list of what to fix now, what to watch and what can wait. If repairs are needed, you get a written price, usually within 24 hours.

Wondering what those repairs might cost? Most home roof repairs run $300–$1,200; see the [roof leak repair cost guide](/cost-to-repair-roof-leak/). If the roof is near the end of its life, the [roof replacement cost guide](/roof-replacement-cost-in-nyc/) shows the full-roof numbers.

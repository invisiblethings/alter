## Why Staten Island flat roofs leak

Flat roofs don't fail at random. On Staten Island homes, almost every leak we trace starts in one of five places:

- **Seams** that have opened or lifted on rubber or TPO
- **Flashing** where the roof meets the house wall, a parapet or a chimney
- **Drains and scuppers** clogged with leaves, so water sits and finds a way in
- **Penetrations** like vent pipes, exhaust fans and AC lines
- **Blisters and splits** in older modified bitumen or tar roofs

Salt air and wind near the water in South Beach, Midland Beach and Great Kills, and freeze-thaw cycles all winter, speed all of this up. We find the real entry point, which is often several feet from the stain on your ceiling, and repair it with materials that match your existing roof. Most of these repairs cost $400–$1,200. See our full [roof leak repair cost guide](/cost-to-repair-roof-leak/) for more detail.

## Flat roof systems we install

### EPDM (rubber)

$5–$12 per sq ft. The best value for most home extensions and garages. Stays flexible through cold winters.

### TPO

$9–$14 per sq ft. A white, heat-welded membrane that reflects sun and keeps the room underneath cooler. See our [TPO roofing guide](/tpo-roofing-guide/).

### Modified bitumen

$9–$13 per sq ft. Multi-layer roofing that stands up to foot traffic, roof decks and AC units.

### PVC

$10–$16 per sq ft. Resists grease and chemicals. Mostly used on restaurants and commercial roofs.

## Repair or replace? The honest answer

**Repair** if the roof is under about 15 years old, the problem is in one area, and the membrane is still flexible.

**Replace** if the roof is near or past 20 years, the insulation underneath is wet over large areas, it leaks in more than one place, or you've already paid for the same repair twice.

When it's close, we give you both prices and photos of what we found, so you can decide with real numbers.

## Signs your roof needs a repair

Call before a small leak becomes a ceiling, insulation and mold problem. These are the signs Staten Island homeowners usually notice first:

- **Brown stains on a ceiling or wall**, often near a chimney, skylight or bathroom vent
- **Missing, cracked or curling shingles** after a nor'easter
- **Granules in the gutters**, the sand-like coating that protects shingles
- **Daylight or damp wood in the attic**
- **Lifted or rusted flashing** around chimneys, walls and vents
- **Water sitting on a flat roof** over an extension or garage two days after rain

If water is coming in right now, skip the rest of this page and [call our emergency line](/roof-repair/emergency-service/).

## What we repair

### Leaks

We scan the roof with the latest infrared (thermal imaging) cameras, which show hidden moisture you can't see with your eyes, and trace water back to where it actually enters, often several feet from the stain on your ceiling. Then we fix the source instead of smearing sealant on the symptom.

### Shingles

Wind-damaged, cracked and missing shingles replaced and matched as closely as possible to the rest of your roof.

### Flashing

Chimney, wall, valley and vent flashing is the most common leak point we find on Staten Island homes. We replace it with new metal.

### Flat roofs

Seams, blisters, punctures and standing water on [EPDM, TPO and modified bitumen roofs](/flat-roofing/) over extensions, garages and attached homes.

### Skylights

Failed seals and flashing around skylights. See [skylight repair](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/).

### Storm damage

Emergency tarping, damage photos for your insurer, then a permanent repair. See [emergency roof repair](/roof-repair/emergency-service/).

## Why Staten Island roofs leak

Nor'easters drive rain sideways under shingles and peel them back, especially on exposed homes in South Beach, Midland Beach and Great Kills. Salt air near the water corrodes flashing faster. Freeze-thaw winters crack old sealant, and heavy summer storms overload gutters and flat-roof drains. A good repair deals with the cause, not just the drip.

## Repair or replace? Our rule of thumb

Repair is usually right when the roof is **under about 15 years old**, the damage is **in one area**, and the shingles or membrane around it are **still flexible**. Replacement makes more sense when the roof is **over 20 years old**, leaks in **several places**, or the deck feels **soft**. When it's close, you get both prices. Leaning toward a new roof?

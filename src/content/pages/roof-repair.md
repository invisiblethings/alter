## Signs your roof needs a repair

Call us before a small leak becomes a ceiling, insulation and mold problem. These are the signs Staten Island homeowners usually notice first:

- **Brown stains on a ceiling or wall**, often near a chimney, skylight or bathroom vent
- **Missing, cracked or curling shingles** after a windy nor'easter
- **Granules in the gutters**, the sand-like coating that protects shingles
- **Daylight or damp wood in the attic**
- **Lifted or rusted flashing** around chimneys, walls and vents
- **Water pooling on a flat roof** for more than two days after rain

If water is coming in right now, skip the rest of this page and [call our emergency line](/roof-repair/emergency-service/).

## What we repair

### Leaks

We trace water back to where it actually enters, which is often several feet from the stain on your ceiling, and fix the source instead of smearing sealant on the symptom.

### Shingles

Wind-damaged, cracked and missing shingles replaced and matched as closely as possible to the rest of your roof.

### Flashing

Chimney, wall, valley and vent flashing is the most common leak point we find on Staten Island homes. We reseal or replace it with new metal.

### Flat roofs

Seams, blisters, punctures and ponding on [EPDM, TPO and modified bitumen roofs](/flat-roofing/) on row homes, garages and extensions.

### Skylights

Failed seals and flashing around skylights. See [skylight repair](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/).

### Storm damage

Emergency tarping, damage photos for your insurer, then a permanent repair. See [emergency roof repair](/roof-repair/emergency-service/).

## How a repair visit works

1. **We inspect the whole roof**, not just the spot you called about, and photograph everything we find.
2. **You get a written quote**, usually within 24 hours, that explains the cause and the fix in plain English.
3. **We do the repair** with materials that match your roof, and clean up after ourselves.
4. **We show you the finished work** with photos and give you a written workmanship warranty.

## Repair or replace? Our honest rule of thumb

We'd rather earn a customer for 20 years than sell one roof. A repair is usually right when:

- The roof is **under about 15 years old**
- The damage is **in one area** (one valley, one chimney, one section)
- The shingles or membrane are **still flexible and intact** around the problem

Replacement usually makes more sense when the roof is **over 20 years old**, leaks in **several places**, or the deck feels **soft or sags**. When it's close, we give you both prices and let you decide. If you're leaning toward a new roof, see [roof replacement in Staten Island](/residential-roofing/roof-replacement/).

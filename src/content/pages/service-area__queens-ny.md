## Queens homes and their roofs

### Detached houses

Bayside, Whitestone, Fresh Meadows, Douglaston and parts of Forest Hills: mostly pitched asphalt shingle roofs.

### Attached rows

Ridgewood, Middle Village, Glendale, Woodside, Sunnyside and Astoria: flat roofs with parapets and shared walls.

### Two-families

Pitched front sections with flat rear roofs are common. We handle both in one job.

### Mixed-use

Storefronts with apartments above along the main avenues. See [commercial roofing](/commercial-roofing/).

## What every NYC roof job needs

**A licensed contractor.** NYC requires a DCWP Home Improvement Contractor license for residential work; ours is #2129904-DCWP. Only licensed contractors can file DOB permits.

**The right permit decision.** Like-for-like reroofing often doesn't need a permit, but deck replacement, roof-type changes, commercial roofs and some full replacements do. Read more in our [NYC roof replacement guide](/roof-replacement-guide-nyc/).

**Code-compliant materials.** Most new and replacement low-slope roofs need a reflective cool-roof surface, and full replacements can trigger Local Laws 92/94 sustainable roofing zones.

**A written contract.** NYC consumer protection rules require written contracts for home improvement work. Ours itemize materials, labor, disposal and permits.

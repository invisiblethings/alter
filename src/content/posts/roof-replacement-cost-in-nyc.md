## Roof replacement cost by borough

The same roof costs different amounts in different boroughs, almost entirely because of access and logistics. Typical full replacement of a ~1,000 sq ft flat roof:

| Borough | Typical range | What drives it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staten Island | $8,000–$13,000 | Detached homes, driveways, easy staging and debris removal |
| Queens | $9,000–$15,000 | Wider streets; mix of flat and pitched residential roofs |
| Brooklyn | $10,000–$17,000 | Row-home access, attached walls, brownstone details, co-op boards |
| Manhattan | $13,000–$22,000+ | Sidewalk protection, cranes or lifts, height, permit complexity |

## What changes the price of your roof

### Size and shape

Price scales with square footage, and with complexity: valleys, dormers, chimneys and skylights all add labor.

### Layers to remove

Many NYC roofs have two or three old layers. Each one adds tear-off and disposal cost.

### Deck condition

Rotted sheathing has to be replaced before the new roof goes on. You won't know the full extent until the old roof is off.

### Access

Driveway or no driveway, attached or detached, building height, and whether materials need hoisting.

### Pitch

Steeper roofs need more safety equipment and take longer.

### Permits & rules

DOB permits ($300–$1,000+ when required), cool-roof requirements, Local Laws 92/94 and co-op or landmark approvals.

## How to budget (and save) on a new roof

- **Inspect early.** Replacing on your schedule is always cheaper than replacing after a ceiling comes down.
- **Repair if you can.** A roof under 15 years old with one problem area usually just needs a [repair](/roof-repair/).
- **Choose the right material, not the most expensive.** Shingles are excellent value on pitched roofs; EPDM or TPO on flat ones.
- **Bundle the work.** Replace aging [gutters](/siding-gutter/gutter-installation-repair/) and [skylights](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/) with the roof to avoid paying for access twice.
- **Compare itemized estimates.** A low total that leaves out disposal, deck repair or permits isn't a low price.

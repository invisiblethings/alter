These are base system prices. Most finished Brooklyn flat roof jobs land between **$10,000 and $17,000** once tear-off of old layers, deck repairs, parapet flashing and access are included.

## What drives the cost of a Brooklyn roof

### Layers of old roofing

Brooklyn roofs often carry two, three or more old layers. Each adds tear-off and disposal.

### Parapets & party walls

New flashing up the parapets and tied into neighbors' roofs is essential, and it's labor-intensive.

### Drainage

Ponding areas may need tapered insulation or crickets, and old drains or scuppers may need replacing.

### Access

No driveway, tight streets and neighbors on both sides mean more hands to move materials and debris.

### Deck repairs

Rotted planks under a long-leaking roof are common and have to be replaced.

### Approvals

Co-op and condo boards, and Landmarks Preservation Commission review in historic districts.

## Brooklyn compared with other boroughs

A ~1,000 sq ft flat roof typically costs **$8,000–$13,000 on Staten Island**, **$9,000–$15,000 in Queens**, **$10,000–$17,000 in Brooklyn** and **$13,000–$22,000+ in Manhattan**. See the full [NYC roof replacement cost guide](/roof-replacement-cost-in-nyc/).

## Repair or replace your Brooklyn roof?

If your roof is under about 15 years old and the leak is at one parapet, seam or drain, a [repair](/service-area/brooklyn-ny/roof-repair/) ($400–$1,200 for most flat roof leaks) is usually the smart move. If it's 20+ years old, has wet insulation over large areas, or keeps leaking in new places, replacement saves money over the next decade. Our guide to [flat roof repair vs. replacement](/flat-roof-repair-and-replacement-in-nyc/) walks through the decision.

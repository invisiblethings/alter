## Pitched roof materials

### Asphalt shingles

The most common roof in Staten Island and Queens for good reason: affordable, durable, easy to repair and available in many colors. Architectural (dimensional) shingles look better and handle wind better than old 3-tab shingles. Learn the differences in [types of asphalt shingles](/types-of-asphalt-shingles/).

### Metal

Standing-seam and metal shingle roofs shed snow, resist wind and can last 40–70 years. A great choice if you plan to stay in your home long-term.

### Slate

The original New York roof on many historic buildings. Lasts a century or more but is heavy, expensive and needs a specialist. Common on brownstones and landmarked homes.

### Tile

Clay and concrete tile suit specific architectural styles and last 50+ years, but the roof structure must be able to carry the weight.

## Flat roof materials

Most Brooklyn, Manhattan and Queens row homes have flat roofs. The main options are **EPDM** (rubber), the affordable workhorse; **TPO**, white and reflective with welded seams (see our [TPO guide](/tpo-roofing-guide/)); **modified bitumen**, multi-ply and tough underfoot; and **PVC**, for restaurants and chemical exposure. Compare them in [flat roof repair vs. replacement](/flat-roof-repair-and-replacement-in-nyc/).

## The best roofing brands

Material brand matters, but **how it's installed matters more**. A great shingle installed badly will fail early. That said, these are the manufacturers most trusted by roofers:

- **Shingles:** GAF, Owens Corning and CertainTeed are the leading US shingle makers. We install GAF as a **GAF Certified®** contractor, which lets us offer GAF system warranties of up to 50 years.
- **Flat roof membranes:** GAF, Firestone (Holcim), Carlisle and Johns Manville. We source TPO, EPDM and modified bitumen systems from these manufacturers.
- **Skylights:** Velux and Fakro are the most common brands we install and service.

When comparing quotes, ask which product line (not just which brand) is included, and whether the installer is certified by that manufacturer.

## How to choose for your home

1. **Roof type:** pitched roofs get shingles, metal, slate or tile; flat roofs get a membrane.
2. **How long you'll stay:** under 15 years, shingles usually win on value; longer, consider metal.
3. **Building and neighborhood:** landmarked buildings may need specific materials; heavy materials need a structural check.
4. **Energy:** a white TPO roof keeps top floors cooler and meets NYC cool-roof rules.

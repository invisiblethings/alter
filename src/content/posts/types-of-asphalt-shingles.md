## The three types of asphalt shingles

### 1. Three-tab shingles

![3-tab shingles](/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/3-Tab-Shingles-GAF-AlterPhaseRoofing.webp)

A single, flat layer of asphalt shingle cut into three equal tabs. They create a uniform, brick-like pattern and were the standard for decades.

- **Pros:** lowest upfront cost, lightweight, simple look
- **Cons:** shorter lifespan, lower wind resistance, flatter appearance, fewer warranty options

### 2. Architectural (dimensional) shingles

![Architectural shingles](/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Architectural-Shingles-GAF-AlterPhaseRoofing.webp)

Two layers bonded together, creating a thicker shingle with depth and shadow lines that look more like wood shake. This is what we install on most homes.

- **Pros:** better wind resistance, longer lifespan, richer appearance, stronger manufacturer warranties
- **Cons:** moderately more expensive than 3-tab

### 3. Luxury (designer) shingles

![Luxury shingles](/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Luxury-Shingles-GAF-AlterPhaseRoofing.webp)

Extra-thick, multi-layer shingles designed to mimic natural slate or cedar shake.

- **Pros:** premium look, heaviest and most durable asphalt shingles
- **Cons:** highest cost of the three

## Side-by-side comparison

| | 3-tab | Architectural | Luxury |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Construction | Single layer | Two laminated layers | Multi-layer, extra thick |
| Look | Flat, uniform | Dimensional, shake-like | Slate or shake look |
| Wind resistance | Lowest | Good | Best |
| Lifespan | Shortest | 20–30 yrs | Longest of the three |
| Cost | Lowest | Moderate | Highest |
| Best for | Tight budgets, sheds | Most homes | High-end and historic looks |

## Which shingle is best for a New York home?

For almost every pitched-roof home on Staten Island, in Queens or in southern Brooklyn, we recommend **architectural shingles**. They handle nor'easter winds better than 3-tab, look better, last longer and come with stronger warranties, for a modest price difference. Luxury shingles are worth it if you want a slate or shake look without the weight and cost of the real thing.

## It's not just the shingles

A shingle roof is a system. What's under the shingles decides how long it lasts: solid [roof sheathing](/roof-sheathing-explained/), synthetic underlayment, ice and water protection at eaves and valleys, proper ventilation, and new flashing.

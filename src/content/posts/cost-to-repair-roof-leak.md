## Roof leak repair costs in NYC (2026)

| Repair | Typical cost | What's usually involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minor leak patch | $300–$650 | Sealing a small hole, nail pop or cracked seal |
| Shingle repair | $350–$800 | Replacing damaged or missing shingles |
| Flat roof leak repair | $400–$1,200 | Seam, blister or puncture on EPDM/TPO/mod-bit |
| Flashing replacement | $450–$900 | Chimney, wall, valley or vent flashing |
| Skylight leak repair | $500–$1,200 | Reflashing or resealing a skylight |
| Gutter / downspout repair | $150–$500 | Overflow pushing water under the roof edge |
| Extensive repair | $1,500–$3,500 | Storm damage, deck repair, chimney flashing rebuild |

Prices are from our own NYC jobs in 2026. Repairs in Manhattan and Brooklyn can run higher than on Staten Island because of access.

## The most common causes of roof leaks (and their fixes)

### Failed flashing

The metal where the roof meets chimneys, walls and vents. The number one leak source we see. Fix: new flashing, $450–$900.

### Damaged shingles

Wind-lifted, cracked or missing shingles let water reach the underlayment. Fix: $350–$800.

### Flat roof seams

Lifted seams, blisters and punctures on row-home roofs. Fix: $400–$1,200.

### Pipe boots & vents

Cracked rubber collars around plumbing vents. One of the cheapest fixes, in the minor-repair range.

### Skylights

Old seals and poorly installed flashing. Fix: $500–$1,200, or replacement. See [skylight repair](/residential-roofing/skylight-installation-repair/).

### Clogged gutters & drains

Water backs up under the roof edge or ponds on flat roofs. Fix: $150–$500 plus any damage.

## What makes a leak repair cost more

- **Hidden damage:** rotted decking or wet insulation under the leak
- **How long it leaked:** months of slow leaks mean more wood and drywall to replace
- **Access:** steep roofs, tall buildings and attached homes with no driveway
- **Matching materials:** older or discontinued shingles take more effort to match
- **Emergency timing:** nights, weekends and active storms

## Repair or replace?

Repair when the roof is **under about 15 years old** and the leak is **in one area**. Consider replacement when the roof is **20+ years old**, leaks in **multiple spots**, has been **patched repeatedly**, or the deck is soft. At that point, repair money is often money spent twice. ## What to do right now if your roof is leaking

1. Move valuables and put a bucket under the drip.
2. If the ceiling bulges with water, a small hole at the lowest point lets it drain safely.
3. Take photos for your records and insurer.
4. Stay off the roof.

## Signs your flat roof needs attention

- **Ponding water** still there 48 hours after rain
- **Blisters or bubbles** in the membrane
- **Cracks, splits or alligatoring** on the surface
- **Lifted seams** or membrane pulling away from walls
- **Damaged flashing** at parapets, curbs and vents
- **Stains on top-floor ceilings**, especially near walls
- **Soft spots** when you walk on it

## When a repair is the right call

A repair is usually right when:

- The roof is **under about 15 years old**
- The problem is **localized**: one seam, one parapet, one drain, one puncture
- The membrane around it is **still flexible and intact**
- The insulation underneath is **dry**

Typical NYC flat roof repairs cost **$400–$1,200**; flashing work runs $450–$900. Larger storm or deck repairs can reach $1,500–$3,500. Before any repair, the real cause should be found. Ponding water, for example, is usually a drainage problem, and patching the membrane without fixing the drain just buys time.

## When it's time to replace

Replacement usually saves money when:

- The roof is **20+ years old**
- **Insulation is wet** over large areas (wet insulation doesn't dry out under a membrane)
- **Leaks keep returning** in new places
- There are **widespread blisters, splits or shrinkage**
- There are already **two or more layers** of old roofing
- You're about to add **solar, a roof deck or HVAC** that would need to come off later

Replacement typically costs about **$8–$14 per sq ft**: EPDM $8–$12, modified bitumen $9–$13, TPO $9–$14, PVC $10–$16. A 1,000 sq ft roof runs about $8,000–$13,000 on Staten Island and $10,000–$17,000 in Brooklyn. See the [Brooklyn roof cost guide](/roof-replacement-cost-in-brooklyn-ny/).

## Choosing a system for your new flat roof

- **EPDM**: most affordable, flexible in the cold, common on row homes
- **TPO**: white and reflective, heat-welded seams, meets cool-roof rules. Read our [TPO guide](/tpo-roofing-guide/).
- **Modified bitumen**: toughest under foot traffic
- **PVC**: best for restaurants and chemical exposure

## What makes NYC flat roofs fail early

Most early flat roof failures we see come from the same few things: **water that can't drain** (clogged drains, sagging decks, layers of old roofing creating low spots), **flashing details done poorly** at parapets and equipment, and **years without an inspection**. Fixing drainage during a replacement, with tapered insulation or crickets, is often the single biggest thing you can do to make a new roof last.

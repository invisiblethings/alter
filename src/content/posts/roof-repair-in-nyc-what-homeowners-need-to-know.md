## Common roof problems by roof type

### Pitched shingle roofs

Most common on Staten Island and Queens houses.

- **Wind damage:** lifted, creased or missing shingles after nor'easters
- **Flashing failures** at chimneys, walls and valleys
- **Granule loss** and curling on aging shingles
- **Ice dams** in cold snaps forcing water under shingles
- **Cracked pipe boots** around plumbing vents

### Flat roofs

Most common on Brooklyn, Manhattan and Queens row homes and commercial buildings.

- **Open seams** on EPDM and TPO
- **Ponding water** from clogged drains or low spots
- **Blisters and cracks** on older membranes
- **Parapet flashing and coping** failures
- **Punctures** from foot traffic and equipment work

## How to spot problems early

From the ground and inside, without climbing up:

- **Ceiling stains** or peeling paint near outside walls
- **Shingles in the yard** or granules piling in gutters
- **Sagging** roof lines
- **Daylight, damp wood or musty smells** in the attic
- **Water standing** on a flat roof two days after rain
- **Overflowing gutters** during rain

Catching these early is the difference between a $400 repair and a $4,000 one. Our [seasonal maintenance checklist](/all-seasons-roofing-staten-island/) shows what to look for each season.

## Repair or replace?

Repair when the roof is **under about 15 years old**, the damage is **localized**, and the materials around it are **still sound**. Replace when it's **20+ years old**, leaks in **multiple places**, or has been patched repeatedly. Flat roof owners can use our [flat roof repair vs. replacement guide](/flat-roof-repair-and-replacement-in-nyc/).

## How to hire a roofer in NYC

Ask how they find leaks. A roofer who uses an infrared (thermal imaging) camera can see hidden moisture in the roof and ceilings, which means fewer guesses and fewer repeat visits. We use one on every leak call.

- **Licensed:** an active NYC DCWP Home Improvement Contractor license
- **Insured:** liability and workers' compensation; ask for certificates
- **Written contract:** required in NYC for home improvement work over $200
- **Explains the cause:** a good roofer shows you photos of what's wrong
- **Warranty in writing** on the repair

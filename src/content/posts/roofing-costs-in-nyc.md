## 🏠 NYC Roof Replacement Costs in 2026

![NYC Roof Replacement Example](/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Roof-Replacement-Cost-NYC-Alter-Phase-Roofing-1024x683.jpg)

Need a new roof? Here’s what full roof replacement costs look like in New York City, based on materials and roof type:

| **Roof Type** | **Cost Per Sq. Ft.** | **Avg. Total (1,200 sq. ft.)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asphalt Shingles | $4.50 – $7.50 | $5,400 – $9,000 |
| Flat Roofing (TPO/EPDM) | $6.00 – $11.00 | $7,200 – $13,200 |
| Modified Bitumen (Torch-Down) | $7.00 – $12.00 | $8,400 – $14,400 |
| Metal Roofing | $10.00 – $16.00 | $12,000 – $19,200 |
| Slate/Tile Roofing | $20.00+ | $24,000+ |

### 💡**Factors that can affect price:**

-   Roof size & pitch
-   Number of layers to remove
-   Permits & code requirements (NYC permits can add $300–$1,000+)
-   Accessibility (brownstones, walk-ups, narrow driveways)
-   Emergency or weekend jobs

## 🔧 NYC Roof Repair Costs in 2026

Not every roofing issue means you need a full replacement. If you’ve got a small leak, cracked flashing, or missing shingles, a repair might be all you need.

Here’s what roof repair typically costs in NYC:

| **Type of Repair** | **Average Cost** |
| --- | --- |
| Minor Leak Patch | $300 – $650 |
| Shingle Repair (Asphalt Roof) | $350 – $800 |
| Flat Roof Leak Repair (TPO/EPDM) | $400 – $1,200 |
| Flashing Replacement | $450 – $900 |
| Skylight Leak Repair | $500 – $1,200 |
| Gutter/Downspout Repairs | $150 – $500 |

Tip: Always get a roof inspected early. Small issues in NYC weather can turn into major damage fast.

* * *

## 💬 Repair vs. Replace: Which One Do You Need?

If your roof is under 15 years old and the damage is localized, a repair usually makes sense. But if it’s leaking in multiple spots, sagging, or over 20 years old, it may be smarter (and cheaper long-term) to replace it.

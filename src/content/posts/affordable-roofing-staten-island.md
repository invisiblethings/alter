## 9 ways to save on your roof (the smart way)

### 1. Repair before you replace

If your roof is under about 15 years old and the problem is in one area, a [repair](/roof-repair/) can buy you years. An honest roofer will tell you when a repair is enough.

### 2. Don't wait on small leaks

A $400 flashing repair today can become a $4,000 job once the deck, insulation and ceiling are soaked.

### 3. Inspect regularly

A yearly check (twice a year for flat roofs) catches cheap fixes early. See our [seasonal roof checklist](/all-seasons-roofing-staten-island/).

### 4. Choose value materials

Architectural shingles on pitched roofs and EPDM on flat roofs give the best cost-per-year. You don't need slate to have a great roof.

### 5. Replace on your schedule

Planned replacements in good weather avoid emergency pricing and rushed decisions.

### 6. Bundle the work

Doing gutters and skylights with the roof avoids paying for setup and access twice.

### 7. Compare itemized estimates

Compare line by line, not totals. A low price that skips tear-off, deck repair or permits isn't a low price.

### 8. Look at insurance

Sudden storm or tree damage may be covered by your homeowners policy. Photos and a written report help.

### 9. Protect the warranty

Hire a manufacturer-certified, licensed installer so a long warranty actually protects you.

## Red flags in a "cheap" roofing quote

- **No license number.** NYC home improvement contractors need an active DCWP license.
- **No proof of insurance.** If an uninsured worker is hurt on your property, you can be liable.
- **A verbal quote.** NYC requires written contracts for home improvement work.
- **Missing line items** like tear-off, disposal, deck repairs or flashing.
- **Roofing over old layers** to save tear-off cost, which shortens the new roof's life.
- **Pressure to sign today.**
